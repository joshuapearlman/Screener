How normalized FCF is calculated

01

Standard FCF

Operating cash flow minus the absolute value of capital expenditure for each annual period.

02

Margin normalized

Aggregate historical FCF divided by aggregate revenue, multiplied by latest annual revenue.

03

Median normalized

Median annual FCF margin multiplied by latest annual revenue to reduce outlier influence.

04

Conservative case

The lower positive estimate, used for valuation only when both methods and six usable periods exist.

Stock-based compensation is shown separately. Banks and insurers are excluded because conventional FCF is not an appropriate primary valuation method for them.
